It has been a busy couple of months, and I am finally able to sit down and type up another blog post. I have been meaning to talk about an app I have grown to really enjoy. [***Dreambeans from Google***](https://labs.google/dreambeans).

Now, I know some people are vehemently opposed to AI. This app is nothing but AI, so you may want to stop reading now. *No offense will be taken.*

I have come to love this app. It connects to your Google Photos and other Google apps, if you give it permission, and creates personalized stories accompanied by AI-generated images of different things and people. It also gives you helpful suggestions about things to do, places to visit, and topics you might find interesting.

<!--more-->

It has captured my likeness remarkably well, though I think it has made me a little older than I am. But that could just be me not wanting to admit I am getting older.

The images are mostly of me, my family, and friends. I save all these images and use them as I see fit, kind of like my own personal Getty Images. But I will say this, my Dreambeans me has better clothes (some of them are actually mine, and others look cool), has more adventures than I do in real life, and reads more books, which I am jealous of. 😆

Dreambeans is for both Android and iOS devices, and it's available for free to eligible users in the United States. If you want some fun and some nice AI-generated images, give it a try. (NOT SPONSORED IN ANY WAY.)

Since it is a Google app, I am not entirely sure how I feel about the privacy implications. You do get to choose which Google apps it can access, and you can delete your Dreambeans data, but it is still worth considering just how much personal information you are allowing an AI application to use.

I have included some samples below. The first one is a real, non-AI photograph of me during Hawaiian Day at work. The rest are images Dreambeans created. They are all in the same style but feature different scenarios, and I think the app does a good job. I hope you enjoy the images and the app if you decide to give it a try.
