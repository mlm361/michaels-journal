One of our two cats has passed away. He was almost 14 years old. We took him to the vet after he stopped eating this weekend and became lethargic. The vet advised that it would probably be best to put him down, as they couldn't guarantee he would recover. He was hypothermic, had fluid around his lungs, and his breathing was labored. We aren't sure exactly what happened, but he will be sorely missed and was greatly loved.

He was fearless. I remember when the dogs he grew up with were getting older, and we were watching a dog for my sister. When he saw my sister's dog being rough with them, he would get between that dog and the dogs he grew up with to protect them. He was protective like that. He was also very playful. When he was younger, he liked to let you pet him for a little while, giving you the impression that everything was fine, and then suddenly swat you with his paw. He never hurt you, but he seemed to think it was funny.

We have one cat left, and after he's gone, which I hope won't be for a long time, I think I'm going to take a break from having animals for a while. I love them, but I've lost quite a few over the last few years, and it doesn't get any easier.
