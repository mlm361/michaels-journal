6. Weather

The sidebar and footer of this blog, and its Weather page, show the weather from my own home weather station in Brandon, Florida. To show it, your browser loads a small script, its styles, a moon picture, and a small file of current readings from my other website, michaelreflects.com.

  • That file holds weather readings and the general place name "Brandon, FL" only. It never includes my address, map position, or any station details.
  • The weather sets no cookies and stores nothing about you. The moon phase is calculated in your browser.
  • The forecast, UV index, and air quality figures come from WeatherAPI.com, but your browser never contacts WeatherAPI.com; my own software at home fetches them.
  • Like any website, michaelreflects.com receives the basic technical details every browser sends with a request, such as your IP address and browser type. The michaelreflects.com Privacy Policy explains how that site handles them.
