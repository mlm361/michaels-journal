This has been some week for hardware and appliance problems in my household.

Thursday, I had a silent hard drive crash. It could have happened before then, but I didn't notice it until Thursday. The drive was there last week, but Windows 11 would not recognize it. I tried it in two different computers to rule out bad cables or a motherboard issue.

I didn't realize the drive was 10 years and 3 months old until I removed it and looked at it. It is kind of like a washer, you don't think about how old it is until something happens. I had a stale backup, so I probably lost 5 to 10 percent of the stuff that was not backed up.
<!--more-->

Then, to add salt to the wound, I got a message from someone on social media. It didn't really bother me, but it kind of did. It seemed like a gentle, polite, maybe funny attempt to sympathize and lighten the situation. At the same time, it was basically saying this is what you get with a 10-year-old drive and that I had it coming. due to its age and that was playing with fire. 

With hard drive shortages and prices going astronomical, I had to buy another drive. I ordered a brand-new 10TB Western Digital drive from Amazon. When it arrived, I noticed it had been manufactured in January 2026. I installed it, but it didn't work. I tested it in two different computers, and it would not initialize at all.

So I started a return through Amazon. They offered me $80 to keep it. I spent well over $300 on the drive, so why would I want $80 to keep one that doesn't work? I need a working drive. I said no.

I know the offer was automated, but come on. Why offer me a discount to keep a dead drive? I need the drive, not the discount.

Today, I received the new drive to replace the replacement drive. It was manufactured in November 2025, and it worked fine. It worked exactly as I expected it should.

While all of this was happening, our upright freezer went on the fritz. We almost lost probably $1,000 worth of meat and frozen vegetables. Now there is another expense and another problem to deal with, all while prices are sky high.

At this point, I can only wonder what will be next.
